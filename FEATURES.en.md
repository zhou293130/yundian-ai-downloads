# Yundian AI Features

[简体中文](FEATURES.md) · [English](FEATURES.en.md) · [Back to overview](README.en.md)

## Automated product listing

- **List from local files on Taobao, Tmall, and Douyin Shop.** Match main images, product details, videos, and SKUs by product folder. Prepare assets and generate titles, attributes, white-background images, or product details as configured. Fill in variants, prices, stock, and fulfillment information, then submit to a supported listing status and record each result.
- **Multi-category templates for Douyin Shop.** Store attributes and SKU rules for multiple categories in one template and apply the corresponding rules to each item in a mixed batch.
- **Miaoshou fully managed TikTok Shop workflows.** Edit items in the collection inbox, prepare English titles and translated product detail images, organize attributes and variants, add videos, packaging, or compliance materials, then save or publish to the selected store.

## Update existing products

- Choose a set of Taobao, Tmall, or Douyin Shop products and update selected fields, including titles, attributes, main images, product details, videos, size charts, SKUs, prices, stock, merchant codes, and shipping settings.
- Taobao and Tmall workflows can check and repair text in main images and preserve the product's sales status during edits. Douyin Shop workflows can save drafts, take listings down, or put them on sale where supported.

## AI titles, images, and product detail content

- **Listing titles and short titles.** Generate titles individually or in bulk from images, categories, existing titles, and keyword rules. Validate length and sync results by product ID. General and women's apparel workflows are available.
- **Detail page planning and editing.** Plan image order, selling points, and visual direction. Generate content by product, revise the plan, regenerate individual images, or mark areas for targeted changes.
- **Main images and product photography.** Create lifestyle images, white backgrounds, studio-style images, virtual try-ons, new poses, and reference-based shots. Batch output by product folder or image, then review, download, or use it in supported listing and editing workflows.

## AI video production and batch editing

- **Narrated videos and batch editing.** Generate or reuse scripts, add voiceovers, captions, covers, and music, and produce one or more videos per product. Batch editing can run locally or in the cloud.
- **Reference-based video creation and editing.** Create clips from text or images, set start and end frames, adapt reference clips, replace a product, use a motion reference, or describe edits in plain language. Browse example shooting styles and use AI to draft shot descriptions.
- **Video templates.** Preview a sample, choose a template, and supply your own assets and product information. Review finished videos before uploading or publishing them.

## Store operations and content publishing

- **Search and recommendation assets.** Find Taobao products that need more assets, then upload matching images and videos in bulk and check the status of each placement.
- **Marketplace trends and opportunities.** Collect trending terms from Taobao and Douyin dashboards and submit eligible products according to match, category, and filter rules.
- **Catalog and video operations.** Track visible products and catalog changes at selected stores. Use supported Taobao Business Manager, Guanghe, and Douyin Shop tools to generate, edit, download, or publish videos. Douyin Shop supports direct publishing and spreadsheet-based product matching for pre-review.

## Asset organization and bulk update tools

- Crop images in bulk, extract video frames for main images, organize product folders, apply detail-page templates, and save available video assets.
- Upload, rename, and link product videos in bulk, collect Taobao video IDs, and update main or detail images through supported Miaoshou or Super Store Manager workflows.
- Organize Taobao and Douyin Shop product spreadsheets and SKU data. AI image review can check for specified text, tags, or surrounding ads and apply targeted corrections as configured.

## Product sourcing and distribution

- Use Xingzuo and Xingqinglian to filter products by market, category, and store assignment. Organize supplier details, product codes, prices, photos, and SKU tables for listing or distribution to multiple stores.
- Prepare assets from Soukuan product links or favorites, or use store and template settings to distribute products to Taobao in bulk and export the results.

## Yundian Agent and MCP

- **Built-in Yundian Agent.** Describe your goal in everyday language. The agent can help organize files, images, and spreadsheets, extract product selling points, prepare copy and task inputs, run authorized tools, inspect results, and follow up on errors.
- **MCP integration.** External agents that support MCP can look up Yundian business rules, settings, and templates, check assets, invoke available tools for listings, editing, and store operations, and read task progress and outputs.

## Task and team management

Save reusable task templates, run tasks across stores within your account limits, monitor results in the Task Center, and find generated images, detail content, and videos in your history. Supported browser tasks can keep running while minimized. Coverage depends on the marketplace, category, and workflow. Check your Account Center for usage quotas.
